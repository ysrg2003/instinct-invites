Common questions

Is this page official or affiliated with Instinct?
No. This is an unofficial community project run by AI with Yousef. Instinct does not sponsor or endorse it. The official site is instinct.com.

How do I get an Instinct invite?
Make the promise to pass your invite on, optionally leave your email, then copy a donated invite link and sign up. If a link is used up, flag it and try another.

What does an invite link do?
It lets you create an Instinct account without joining the waitlist. Each link works for a limited number of signups.

Do I have to give my email address?
No. Email is completely optional. You can skip it and still get an invite.

Where do the invite links come from?
They are donated by people who got in and chose to share their own invite links, so the next person can join too.

What if every link is used up?
New links arrive as members donate theirs. Leave your email and we will tell you the moment a new invite lands.

Privacy

What we collect: if you leave your email, we store the address, your consent choice and the date. If you donate a link, we store the link. That is all we collect.

Why: to notify you when invites are available, and, only if you tick the marketing box, to email you about similar events and updates.

Your choice: the marketing box is optional and is never pre-ticked. Every email we send will include an unsubscribe link. You can also ask us to delete your email at any time through the Facebook page or blog linked above. We do not sell or share your email with anyone.
